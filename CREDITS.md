# Credits

## Shawn Noel (Shawn64)

Every game in this archive was created by Shawn Noel, working as Shawn64's Creations and later S64 Games. He did
the graphics, programming and design, voiced Max Nitro in Street Bike Fury, and ran Shawn64.com and
S64Games.com, where the games were shared.

## Street Bike Fury (S64 Games, 2005)

As credited in the game:

- Creator: **Shawn Noel**. Graphics, programming, design.
- Programming support: **Brandon Rohrer**. Level generation programming. (The `room_store` and `room_set` scripts
  that power the endless levels carry his 2005 notice.)
- Voice acting: **Shawn Noel** as Max Nitro, **Dave Perritte** as the Enemy, **Aaron Brimm** as the Drug Dealer.
- Programming tools: **Mark Overmars** (Game Maker).
- Testing team: **Jorge Hernandez**, **Brandon Rohrer**, **Dave Perritte**.
- Additional support: S64 Games community members, Game Maker community members, and the Shangri-La staff (Graal Online).
- Music in the original release: three songs by Staind (not included in this archive).
